Region: lambda_1 ∈ [5, 12],
        lambda_2 ∈ [10, 50],
        lambda_3 ∈ [30, 90],
        gamma/beta non-critical (gamma = 8, beta = 1 used)
        
        candidate point (6.79, 20.5, 29.95) confirmed on 200 fresh runs.

Claim: in this region, the community-based model matches the classical QUBO's risk (Δ ≈ 0.003) and return (Δ ≈ 0.015)
    at noise level — but via a measurably different portfolio (L1 ≈ 0.38 vs a 0.06 noise floor),
    reflecting the cross-community covariance the decomposition cannot see.

    For candidate point, community–classical L1 ≈ 0.38 versus a classical self-distance of 0.061.
    The classical annealer reproduces its own portfolio to within ~3% of total mass across independent runs,
    so the noise floor is tight — and the community model sits 6× above it.
    L1 of 0.38 means roughly 19% of the portfolio's mass is allocated to different assets - a systematically different portfolio.

    The community pipeline finds a portfolio that is statistically equivalent to the classical one:
        same risk to ~0.003, same return to ~0.015 — but it is a different portfolio, redistributing about a fifth of the weight.

Boundaries: closeness degrades sharply for lambda_1 ≳ 20 (return-chasing regimes) and lambda_3 ≲ 25;
            lambda_2 ≲ 10 additionally causes budget-violation artifacts including whole-community dropout.
            
    Note:   Community dropout is when an entire community's inner solve returns all-zero bits and that community is then
            zeroed out of the portfolio

Merging: ~20% of runs find 4 communities;
        effect on closeness ~10% per event, ~2% aggregate;
        insensitive to gamma, so likely genuine 4-community structure in the data.

Tuning Lambdas further: Further tuning at this point would be ranking noise and then zooming into it
    The only two things that would legitimately reopen the lambda question, for the record:
        
        Moving to the actual QPU - Hardware noise, embedding, and chain breaks change the effective solution quality,
        and QUBO penalty balance interacts with all of that.
        The region likely transfers roughly, but "roughly" is worth a small (say 32-point) sweep on hardware before certifying
        it there — not because the current analysis is wrong, but because it was conditioned on neal at 400 reads / 300 sweeps,
        which is baked into the data.

        Changing the problem - Different asset universe, different number of communities, different bits_per_asset:
            Any of these shifts the QUBO's scale structure, and lambda_2/lambda_3 balance is scale-dependent.
            The region is a property of this configuration, not a universal constant.
